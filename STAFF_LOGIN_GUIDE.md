# Staff Login Guide for ISNM School Management System

## Overview
This document explains the login process for all staff members of the ISNM School Management System.

## Login Credentials
To access the system, staff members require:
- **Email**: Your official ISNM email address (format: `username@isnm.ug`)
- **Password**: Your secure password

### Default Password
For newly created staff accounts, the default password is: `12345678`
**Important**: You must change your password on first login for security reasons.

## Login Process
1. Navigate to the staff login page (typically `login.php` or similar)
2. Enter your ISNM email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click the "Login" button

## Security Features

### Account Lockout
To protect against brute-force attacks:
- After **5 consecutive failed login attempts**, your account will be temporarily locked
- Lockout duration: **15 minutes**
- During lockout, you will see the message: "Account temporarily locked due to multiple failed attempts. Please try again later."

### Password Requirements
While the system accepts the default password, it is strongly recommended to:
- Use a strong, unique password
- Change your password regularly
- Never share your password with anyone

## Role-Based Access
After successful login, you will be redirected to a dashboard specific to your role. The system supports various staff roles including:
- Director General / CEO
- HR Manager
- Bursar / School Bursar
- Academic Registrar
- School Principal
- Director of Academics/Finance/ICT
- Head of Nursing/Midwifery
- Deputy Principal
- School Secretary
- Lecturers/Senior Lecturers
- Sickbay, Matrons, Wardens
- Non-teaching staff
- Drivers, Security, School Librarian

Your role determines which modules and information you can access.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
1. **Invalid email or password**:
   - Double-check your email format (should end with `@isnm.ug`)
   - Ensure Caps Lock is off
   - Verify you're using the correct password

2. **Account locked**:
   - Wait 15 minutes before trying again
   - Contact system administrator if you need immediate access

3. **Email not recognized**:
   - Confirm you're using your official ISNM email
   - Contact HR to verify your account is active and properly configured

## Password Change
To change your password:
1. Log in to the system
2. Navigate to your profile/settings page
3. Select "Change Password"
4. Enter your current password and new password twice
5. Save changes

## Logging Out
Always log out when you finish your session, especially on shared computers:
- Click your profile icon or name in the top-right corner
- Select "Logout" from the dropdown menu
- Confirm the logout action

## Support
For login issues or account problems, contact:
- **IT Support**: it-support@isnm.ug
- **HR Department**: hr@isnm.ug
- **System Administrator**: admin@isnm.ug
